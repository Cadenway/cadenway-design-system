Add the `@cadenway/ui/components/page-header` entry point: `cw-page-header` (`size` `default` or `compact`) with its parts `cw-page-header-title` (the page's `h1`), `cw-page-header-favorite` (`[(favorite)]`, `ariaLabel` « Favori », `aria-pressed`), `cw-page-header-description`, `cw-page-header-actions` and `cw-page-header-tabs`, which renders the projected `cw-tabs` in the `bar` variant with the « + Vue » area after it. Without tabs the default header draws its own bottom rule; the compact header puts everything on one 44 px row.
